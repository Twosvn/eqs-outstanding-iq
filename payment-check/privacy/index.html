How Payment Check uses your information

Payment Check is operated by Fynrec. Contact hello@fynrec.co.uk for account, correction or data requests.

Your account

We use your email address and business name to provide an account, confirm your email and contact you about your submissions. Supabase Auth manages passwords and account verification. Sign-in sessions use secure, HttpOnly cookies; authentication tokens are not kept in browser local storage.

Reports and evidence

We collect the company being reported, invoice dates, payment terms, your payment experience and publication confirmations. A first-hand report requires a supporting PDF, PNG or JPEG. Redact bank details and unnecessary personal information before uploading. Evidence is stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket and can be downloaded only through authorised account or moderation requests.

Private review, then publication

Account and submission data are stored in Supabase. Netlify hosts the site and its backend functions. After review, we may publish your business name, the reported company’s name and number, invoice terms and dates, payment outcome, headline and account of the experience. Approved company responses can be published alongside a report. Your account email, evidence and moderation correspondence are excluded from public reports.

Links to other sites

External complaint links, your connection to the source and the payment-specific note are held for private assessment. They are not automatically fetched, copied, published or included in payment statistics. We seek the original reviewer’s confirmation, evidence and publication permission and check source restrictions before considering a first-hand review.

Corrections and withdrawals

You can view and withdraw your own submissions in your account. Withdrawal removes them from Fynrec’s public report pages. A reviewed business can submit a correction, resolution update or removal request. Public links may already have been shared or indexed by external services. Contact us to request account deletion or review retention of private records.

Separate from debt recovery

An account, report or response does not instruct Fynrec to recover a debt. Any recovery work requires a separate agreement. Payment Check has no account subscription.
