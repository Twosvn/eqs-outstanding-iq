How Payment Check submissions are used

Payment Check is operated by Fynrec, a trading name of Equisettle Ltd.

What you submit

We collect your business name, work email, the company being reviewed, invoice dates, payment experience and confirmations. For company responses, we also collect your role and the review reference where supplied.

Private review, then publication

Submissions are received through Netlify Forms, our hosting provider. Your work email and correspondence are used to assess the submission and contact you privately. Do not submit invoice documents, bank details or personal information about individuals in these forms.

After review, we may publish your business name, the company name and number, payment terms and dates, outcome, review headline and experience. Approved company responses may be published alongside reviews. Email addresses are not included in published payment records.

Corrections and removal requests

You can request a correction, removal or resolution update using the response form. We assess the request and may contact you to verify your identity or authority.

Separate from debt recovery

Submitting a review does not instruct us to recover a debt. Responding to reviews and requesting corrections do not require purchasing a recovery service.

See Fynrec’s privacy policy for wider information about data handling and your rights.
