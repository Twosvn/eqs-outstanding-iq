Payment Check account terms

Use your account to submit first-hand reports about late or unpaid business invoices, share external source links for private assessment, and respond for a business you are authorised to represent.

Accurate, supported reports

Submit accurate information based on a real business transaction. Reviews require private supporting evidence and your permission to publish the business name and account you submit. Do not impersonate a business or include unnecessary personal information, confidential bank details or abusive content.

Review before publication

Submissions are private until assessed. We may request more information, decline publication, correct a record or remove content. A submission is not a finding that a debt is legally due. External links are assessed separately and are not automatically copied or published.

Your account and reports

Keep your sign-in details private. You can view and withdraw your submissions in your account. Use the company response form for corrections or resolution updates. Evidence is accessible to its submitter and authorised moderators, not the public.

Separate from recovery services

An account or report does not instruct Fynrec to collect a debt. Any recovery work requires a separate agreement. There is no subscription for Payment Check accounts.

Read how we use your information. For account or data requests contact hello@fynrec.co.uk.
